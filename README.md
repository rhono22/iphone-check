# iPhone Check

A one-page web app for inspecting a (second-hand) iPhone before buying: open it in Safari on the iPhone you are checking.

**Tests:** touchscreen dead spots, multi-touch, display colors (dead pixels, burn-in), speaker, microphone,
back and front camera, motion sensors and compass, GPS, Wi-Fi and mobile data.

**Settings checklist:** Find My / iCloud signed out, battery health, Parts and Service History (replaced battery
or screen), serial and IMEI, Face ID, True Tone, buttons, charging, calls, SIM/carrier lock, Bluetooth, camera lenses,
physical damage.

Ends with a report you can share or copy. Nothing is uploaded; everything runs on the phone.

Note: no app or web page can read battery health, replaced parts or the serial number on iOS; those are checked in
Settings, and the checklist tells you where to look.
